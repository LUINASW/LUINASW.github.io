Streamclipssy Clip Tool

Streamclipssy publishes short highlight clips from GTA 6 streams, made with the permission of the streamers featured.

The Streamclipssy Clip Tool is the software that produces and posts those clips. It runs on the channel owner's own computer and:

  • finds entertaining moments in streams the creators have authorized us to use,
  • edits them into vertical videos of 30 to 60 seconds with captions and credit to the creator,
  • publishes them to Streamclipssy's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook through each platform's official API.

The tool only posts to accounts owned by Streamclipssy. It doesn't post on behalf of anyone else, and it doesn't collect information from viewers.

Credit to creators

Every clip names the streamer it features. If you're a creator and want a clip removed, or want to change what we may use, contact us (details below) and we'll act promptly.
