Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what data the Streamclipssy Clip Tool (“the Tool”) handles. The Tool is used only by the owner of Streamclipssy to publish clips to Streamclipssy's own social media accounts.

Who uses the Tool

Only the Streamclipssy channel owner. The Tool doesn't offer accounts or sign-in to the public, and it doesn't post on behalf of other people.

Data we access through platform APIs

When the owner connects an account (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook), the platform grants the Tool permission to:

  • upload and publish videos to that account,
  • read basic account information (such as the channel or account ID) and the status of uploaded videos,
  • read public performance figures for our own videos (such as views and likes), to learn which clips work best.

We don't access viewers' personal data, private messages, contacts or followers' information.

How data is stored

  • Access tokens issued by the platforms are stored only on the owner's computer, in the Windows Credential Manager.
  • Video files, clip records and performance figures are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • We don't sell, rent or share this data with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

YouTube

The Tool uses YouTube API Services. By using content we publish on YouTube you're also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can disconnect any platform at any time, which deletes its stored tokens from the computer. Platform permissions can also be removed from each platform's own settings page. To ask about data or request deletion, contact us below.

Creators featured in clips

Clips feature streamers who have given permission. A creator can ask for a clip to be removed or for their permission to be withdrawn at any time by contacting us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
